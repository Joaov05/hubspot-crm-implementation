## Pipelines

### New business (deals)

Every new customer opportunity, from first discovery call to signature.

| # | Stage | Probability | Entry criteria | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Discovery | 10% | SQL accepted and discovery meeting booked | Pain, current tool and monthly shipments recorded |
| 2 | Demo | 25% | Discovery done and the customer agreed to see the platform | Demo held and primary pain recorded |
| 3 | Technical validation | 40% | Customer IT or operations reviewing integrations (ERP, e-commerce) | Integration path confirmed and implementation effort estimated |
| 4 | Proposal | 60% | Decision maker identified and proposal sent | Proposal reviewed with the decision maker |
| 5 | Negotiation | 80% | Customer negotiating terms or in legal review | Contract signed or deal lost |
| 6 | Closed won | 100% | Contract signed. Creates the onboarding ticket | — |
| 7 | Closed lost | 0% | Customer declined. Closed lost category is required | — |

### Expansion and renewal (deals)

Upsell and renewal opportunities on existing customers, owned by CS and AEs.

| # | Stage | Probability | Entry criteria | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opportunity identified | 20% | Upsell signal or renewal within 90 days | Customer confirms interest or renewal conversation is booked |
| 2 | Proposal sent | 50% | Commercial proposal sent | Customer gives feedback on the proposal |
| 3 | Negotiation | 75% | Terms being discussed | Signed or lost |
| 4 | Won | 100% | Contract signed. Current MRR and renewal date are updated on the company | — |
| 5 | Lost | 0% | Customer declined the upsell or did not renew | — |

### Customer onboarding (tickets)

From closed won to a customer who uses the platform on their own. Target is go-live in 30 days.

| # | Stage | State | Entry criteria | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kickoff scheduled | OPEN | Created automatically when a New business deal is closed won | Kickoff meeting held (SLA 2 business days) |
| 2 | Integration setup | OPEN | Access granted and integration plan agreed | ERP / e-commerce integration tested |
| 3 | Training | OPEN | Integration working in test | Operations team trained |
| 4 | Go-live | OPEN | First real shipments quoted on the platform | Two weeks of stable usage |
| 5 | Onboarding complete | CLOSED | Customer handed over to the CSM for ongoing success | — |
| 6 | Onboarding cancelled | CLOSED | Customer cancelled before go-live | — |

## Permission matrix

| Team | Contacts | Companies | Deals | Tickets | Settings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marketing | edit all | view all | view all | none | none |
| SDR | edit team | edit team | view all | none | none |
| Sales | edit own | edit own | edit own | view all | none |
| Customer Success | view all | edit all | edit team | edit all | none |
| RevOps | edit all | edit all | edit all | edit all | edit all |

- Only RevOps creates or edits properties, pipelines and workflows (super admin).
- Exporting records is allowed for RevOps and team leads only.
- Sales sees all deals in read-only mode, so the pipeline review uses one shared view.
- Deleting records is restricted to RevOps; everyone else archives or marks as Other.
